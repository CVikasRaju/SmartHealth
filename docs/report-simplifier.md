# Medical Report Simplifier Specification

## 1. Purpose & Scope

The **Medical Report Simplifier** bridges communication gaps between complex diagnostic lab output and patient comprehension[cite: 16]. It converts raw lab data into plain-language summaries while enforcing strict clinical disclaimers against diagnostic speculation[cite: 16].

---

## 2. Pipeline Architecture

```text
[ Upload PDF / Image ] 
        │
        ▼
[ Preprocessing (Binarization, Denoising) ]
        │
        ▼
[ Simulated OCR text engine (see "Extraction status" below) ]
        │
        ▼
[ Biomarker Regex & Named-Entity Matcher ]
        │
        ▼
[ Normalization & Reference Range Scoring ]
        │
        ▼
[ Plain-Language Translation & History Linking ]
        │
        ▼
[ Exportable Summary with Non-Diagnostic Disclaimer ]
```

---

## 3. Standard Dictionary & Biomarker Normalization

The system recognizes and maps routine panels without clinical bias[cite: 16]:

| Normalized Key | Matched Aliases | Standard Unit | Normal Range | Plain-Language Educational Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `fbs` | Fasting Blood Sugar, Glucose Fasting | mg/dL | 70 – 99 | Measures blood sugar after fasting. Evaluates how the body manages glucose fuel[cite: 16]. |
| `hba1c` | Glycated Hemoglobin, A1C | % | 4.0 – 5.6 | Reflects average blood sugar control over the past 2 to 3 months[cite: 16]. |
| `creatinine` | Serum Creatinine, Creat | mg/dL | 0.7 – 1.3 | A natural waste product from muscle wear; filters through kidneys into urine. |
| `platelets` | Platelet Count, PLT | ×10³/µL | 150 – 450 | Tiny blood cell fragments that help blood clot and stop bleeding. |
| `hemoglobin` | Hb, Hgb | g/dL | 13.5 – 17.5 | The oxygen-carrying protein in red blood cells that transports air from lungs. |
| `tsh` | Thyroid Stimulating Hormone | µIU/mL | 0.4 – 4.0 | A master hormone from the pituitary gland that regulates your metabolism. |

---

## 4. Longitudinal Historical Tracking

When a patient uploads multiple reports over time, the system indexes the biomarker by `(patientId, normalizedKey, reportDate)`:
- Renders an interactive historical line chart comparing previous values[cite: 16].
- Shows whether a biomarker is trending upward, downward, or remaining stable across visits[cite: 16].
- Never declares a prognosis; visually contrasts the values against normal physiological reference boundaries[cite: 16].

---

## 5. Mandatory Medical Disclaimer

Every generated patient summary, screen, and downloaded PDF must display this immutable notice:

> **IMPORTANT MEDICAL DISCLAIMER**:  
> *This document is an educational explanation generated by SmartMedic AI to explain clinical terms, values, and reference ranges in everyday language. It does not provide medical diagnoses, treatment recommendations, or clinical prognoses. Lab values must always be evaluated in the context of your complete medical history by your attending physician. Do not start, stop, or modify any medication without consulting your doctor.*[cite: 16]
